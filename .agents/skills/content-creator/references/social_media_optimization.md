## 2. Platform-Specific Strategies

### A. LinkedIn Strategy (B2B & Thought Leadership)
- **The "See More" Hook**: The first 2-3 lines before the fold dictate 80% of click-through. Never start with "I'm excited to announce". Start with a provocative observation, hard metric, or vulnerability.
- **Formatting**:
  - Double line breaks between short sentences.
  - Bullet points (`•`, `→`, `✓`) for lists.
  - Keep external links in the first comment or edit them into the body after 10 minutes to avoid algorithmic reach penalties.
- **Document Posts (PDF Carousels)**:
  - Generate 3-5x higher engagement than static image posts.
  - Recommended size: 1080x1350 (4:5 ratio) or 1080x1080 square.
  - 6 to 10 slides per carousel. Slide 1 must be a magazine-cover style title.

### B. X / Twitter Strategy
- **Thread Architecture**:
  - Tweet 1: Hook + Stake + Credibility + Call to read ("Here's the playbook:").
  - Tweets 2 through N-1: 1 actionable idea per tweet. Avoid fluff.
  - Final Tweet: Summary table + clear CTA (Retweet original + follow + newsletter link).
- **Engagement Triggers**:
  - Ask open questions at the end ("What did I miss?").
  - Reply to the first 5-10 comments within the first 30 minutes of posting.

### C. Instagram Strategy
- **Reels (Reach)**:
  - 3-second hook: visual movement + on-screen caption hook.
  - Keep pacing fast (cut every 2-3 seconds).
  - Use trending audio or clear, crisp voiceover with subtitles.
- **Carousels (Retention & Saves)**:
  - High saves = high algorithmic distribution.
  - Make slides "save-worthy" (cheatsheets, step-by-step guides, resource stacks).
  - Slide 10: "Save this post for your next campaign".

## 3. The 3-Tier Hashtag Architecture

Avoid stuffing 30 generic tags. Use this 5-tag formula:

1. **Broad Industry Tag** (1M+ posts): `#Marketing` or `#SaaS`
2. **Niche Topic Tag** (100k - 500k posts): `#ContentMarketing` or `#SEOstrategy`
3. **Micro-Community Tag** (10k - 100k posts): `#B2BMarketing` or `#CopywritingTips`
4. **Specific Problem Tag** (1k - 10k posts): `#OrganicTrafficGrowth`
5. **Brand / Campaign Tag**: `#YourBrandName`

## 4. Social Media Pre-Publish Checklist

Before hitting publish on any social asset:
- [ ] Hook placed within first 2 lines (before the cutoff / fold)
- [ ] Optimal aspect ratio verified (1:1, 4:5, or 9:16)
- [ ] Mobile-tested formatting (no giant blocks of unspaced text)
- [ ] Direct engagement question or poll included
- [ ] All tagged handles verified and active
- [ ] Relevant link tested (in bio, comment, or dedicated CTA)
