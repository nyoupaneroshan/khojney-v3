## In-Depth Review of Top Hospitals in Nepal

### 1. Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital (TUTH), Maharajgunj
Established under the Institute of Medicine (IOM) in 1982, TUTH is widely regarded as the cornerstone of public healthcare in Nepal. It combines advanced clinical services with clinical research and doctor training.
- **Key Departments**: General Medicine, Cardiology, Liver & Kidney Transplantation, Neurology, Orthopedics.
- **Why Choose TUTH**: Highly affordable government-subsidized rates combined with some of the most senior medical professors and surgeons in the country.
- **Emergency Services**: Dedicated 24/7 Emergency and Trauma wing with triage management.

### 2. Nepal Mediciti Hospital, Bhaisepati, Lalitpur
Nepal Mediciti is one of the largest private healthcare institutions in South Asia, accredited by NABH. With rooftop helipads for emergency air-ambulance evacuation, it represents the gold standard in private infrastructure.
- **Key Departments**: Comprehensive Cancer Care, Joint Replacement, Interventional Cardiology, Robotic Surgery.
- **Why Choose Mediciti**: Comprehensive modern diagnostic equipment (3T MRI, 128 Slice CT), paperless digital health records, and premium patient suites.

### 3. Patan Hospital (Patan Academy of Health Sciences), Lalitpur
Located in Lagankhel, Patan Hospital provides compassionate, high-volume care to thousands of daily outpatients. It is widely praised for maternal care, pediatrics, and infectious disease management.
- **Key Features**: Clean, patient-centric environment with transparent diagnostic pricing and emergency services. Accepts government [health insurance schemes](/insurance).

### 4. Shahid Gangalal National Heart Center, Bansbari
As Nepal's premier specialized cardiac institute, Gangalal provides subsidized and free cardiac surgeries for pediatric patients and senior citizens.
- **Key Departments**: Coronary Angiography, Open Heart Surgery, Valve Replacement, Pediatric Cardiology.
- **Emergency Capabilities**: 24/7 emergency cath lab for acute myocardial infarction (heart attack) patients.

### 5. Grande International Hospital, Dhapasi
Grande International Hospital is designed to provide international standard tertiary care within Kathmandu Valley.
- **Key Departments**: Orthopedics and Sports Medicine, Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery, Neurosciences, and Critical Care Medicine.
- **Helipad Facility**: Direct rooftop emergency medical evacuation from remote trekking regions and mountain districts.

## Government vs Private Hospitals in Nepal: How to Decide?

When choosing where to seek medical attention, consider these factors:

| Consideration | Government & Teaching Hospitals | Private Multi-Specialty Hospitals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost of Care** | Very economical; covered by Government Swasthya Bima | Higher out-of-pocket costs; requires private insurance |
| **Wait Times** | Longer queues for OPD consultations and elective surgeries | Minimal waiting; quick scheduling and digital appointments |
| **Infrastructure** | High patient volume; functional amenities | Premium private rooms, cafeteria, valet, international equipment |
| **Doctors** | Highly experienced senior professors and department heads | Experienced consultants with flexible consultation hours |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which hospital is best for emergency trauma care in Kathmandu?
The **National Trauma Center** (adjacent to Bir Hospital in Mahabouddha) and the **TUTH Emergency Wing** in Maharajgunj operate round-the-clock emergency surgical and ICU facilities equipped for severe multi-trauma accidents.

### Do hospitals in Nepal accept government health insurance (Swasthya Bima)?
Yes. Government and community hospitals such as Patan Hospital, Bir Hospital, TUTH, and Civil Hospital accept the Government Swasthya Bima card for subsidized consultations, admissions, and medicines.
