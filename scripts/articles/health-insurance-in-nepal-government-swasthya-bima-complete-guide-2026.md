# Health Insurance in Nepal: Government Swasthya Bima, Private Policies & Claims 2026

Unexpected medical emergencies and escalating hospitalization expenses can disrupt family finances. In Nepal, securing reliable **health insurance in Nepal** has expanded through the **Government Health Insurance Board (Swasthya Bima Board - स्वास्थ्य बीमा बोर्ड)** alongside comprehensive private health and critical illness policies offered by non-life and life insurance companies.

This guide provides an overview of health insurance in Nepal: how the government's subsidized Swasthya Bima scheme works, who is eligible, which hospitals accept the card, and how to purchase private corporate or family health coverage.

---

## Understanding Nepal's Health Insurance Landscape

Healthcare coverage in Nepal is categorized into two primary frameworks:

1. **Government Health Insurance Program (Swasthya Bima)**: A social security program run by the Health Insurance Board (HIB) under the Ministry of Health and Population, aimed at making healthcare accessible to all citizens regardless of income.
2. **Private Medical Insurance**: Offered by licensed general (non-life) insurance companies providing higher coverage limits (NPR 5 Lakhs to NPR 25+ Lakhs), cashless hospitalization, private cabin admissions, and international treatment coverage.

## The Government Health Insurance Scheme (Swasthya Bima)

### 1. Contribution & Coverage Breakdown

| Family Size | Annual Premium (Contribution) | Maximum Annual Coverage | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to 5 Members** | **NPR 3,500** per year | **NPR 1,00,000** | Standard family package |
| **Additional Members (Above 5)** | **NPR 700** per extra person | **NPR 20,000** additional per person | Max coverage capped at NPR 2,00,000 |
| **Senior Citizens (Age 70+)** | **100% Free** (Government subsidized) | **NPR 1,00,000** | Individual coverage funded by state |
| **Ultra-Poor / Disabled / HIV+** | **100% Free** | Full Standard Benefit | Verified through local government cards |

### 2. What Does Swasthya Bima Cover?
- **Outpatient Care (OPD)**: General doctor consultations, specialist checkups, and diagnostic lab tests.
- **Inpatient Care (IPD)**: Hospital admission, bed charges (general ward), surgeries, and nursing fees.
- **Essential Medicines**: Over 1,000+ government-listed essential generic medicines are provided free of cost at the hospital pharmacy.
- **Emergency Services**: Emergency room treatment and urgent stabilizing procedures.

### 3. What is NOT Covered?
- Cosmetic and plastic surgeries
- Highly expensive non-essential dental procedures (except emergency extractions)
- Expensive corrective spectacles (limited subsidy only)
- Self-inflicted injuries or substance-abuse rehabilitation

## How to Enroll in Government Health Insurance (Step-by-Step)

Enrolling in the Swasthya Bima program is handled at the local ward level:

1. **Contact Your Local Ward Enrollment Assistant**: Every local municipality/ward has an assigned **Darta Sahayogi (दर्ता सहयोगी)**.
2. **Submit Required Documentation**:
   - Citizenship certificates of all adult family members.
   - Birth certificates for children under 16.
   - Recent passport-size photographs of all family members.
3. **Select Your Primary Health Point (First Point of Contact)**: You must designate an authorized local health post, primary health center, or government hospital as your initial service point.
4. **Pay the Annual Contribution**: Pay NPR 3,500 for up to 5 family members.
5. **Receive Your Digital Insurance Card**: Once active, you present your card and referral slip when seeking medical attention at participating [partner hospitals](/hospitals).

## Private Health Insurance vs Government Swasthya Bima

For individuals seeking higher sum insured amounts, private rooms, or coverage in top private hospitals:

| Parameter | Government Swasthya Bima | Private Health Insurance Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sum Insured** | NPR 1,00,000 to NPR 2,00,000 | NPR 3,00,000 to NPR 25,00,000+ |
| **Annual Cost** | Fixed NPR 3,500 per family | NPR 8,000 to NPR 35,000+ (Age/coverage based) |
| **Hospital Choice** | Government, teaching, and designated community hospitals | Cashless across top private hospitals (Mediciti, Grande, Norvic) |
| **Pre-existing Illnesses**| Covered from day one | Subject to waiting periods (1 to 2 years) |
| **Room Entitlement** | General Ward | Private Single Cabin or Deluxe Room |

## Step-by-Step Hospital Claim Process in Nepal

### 1. Cashless Hospitalization (Direct Billing)
1. Present your Health Insurance Card / Policy Number at the hospital's Insurance Helpdesk upon admission.
2. The hospital desk coordinates directly with the insurer / Swasthya Bima Board for claim pre-authorization.
3. Upon discharge, you only pay for non-covered incidentals (e.g., food, premium hygiene kits), while covered medical bills are settled directly.

### 2. Reimbursement Claims (If Cashless is Unavailable)
1. Collect all original hospital discharge summaries, diagnostic reports, and itemized bills.
2. Complete the insurer's formal claim application form.
3. Submit the claim file to the insurance company within 15–30 days of hospital discharge.
4. The claim payout is disbursed directly into your bank account following medical audit.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I choose any hospital directly under Government Swasthya Bima?
In non-emergency scenarios, you must first visit your designated **First Service Point (Primary Hospital)**. If specialized treatment is required, they will issue a formal **Referral Slip (प्रेषण पत्र)** enabling you to visit central tertiary centers like Patan Hospital or TUTH.

### Does health insurance cover family members living outside Kathmandu?
Yes. The Government Health Insurance Card is tied to the family unit and can be utilized across registered government referral centers nationwide.
