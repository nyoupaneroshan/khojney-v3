# How to Apply for E-Passport in Nepal Online 2026: Application, Documents & Fees

The Government of Nepal, through the **Department of Passports (DoP - राहदानी विभाग)** under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, issues second-generation biometric **Electronic Passports (e-Passports)** to Nepali citizens. Featuring an embedded electronic microprocessor chip holding biometric and cryptographic data, the Nepali e-Passport enhances security and expedites immigration clearance worldwide.

Because submitting an **e-passport Nepal online application** now strictly requires a verified **National Identity Card Number (Rastriya Parichayapatra / NID)**, understanding the prerequisite steps is essential to avoid booking delays and application rejections.

This guide provides a walkthrough on booking your online appointment, gathering documents, paying application fees, and collecting your passport.

---

## Prerequisites: The Mandatory National ID (NID) Requirement

Under current Ministry of Foreign Affairs directives, **all applicants aged 16 and above must possess a verified National Identity Number (NID)** before applying for an ordinary e-Passport.

### How to Obtain Your NID Number in Nepal:
1. Visit the Department of National ID and Civil Registration portal ([nidmc.gov.np](https://nidmc.gov.np)).
2. Complete the online pre-registration form with personal and family lineage details.
3. Book an appointment at your local District Administration Office (DAO) or designated registration station in Kathmandu (e.g., Rastriya Sabha Griha, Narayanhiti).
4. Provide physical biometrics (10 fingerprints, iris scan, digital photograph).
5. You will receive an immediate SMS containing your **10-digit National Identity Card Number (NIN)**, which is entered into the e-Passport application portal.

## Step-by-Step: Online E-Passport Application Walkthrough

Follow this step-by-step process to apply online:

### Step 1: Open the Official Application Portal
Navigate to [emrtds.nepalpassport.gov.np](https://emrtds.nepalpassport.gov.np) and select **Apply for Passport**. Choose your application category:
- *First Issuance* (First time applicant)
- *Renewal* (Expiring machine-readable or e-passport)
- *Replacement* (Lost, stolen, or damaged passport)

### Step 2: Select Appointment Location and Date
Choose between your local **District Administration Office (DAO)**, an **Area Administration Office**, or the central **Department of Passports (Tripureshwor, Kathmandu)**. Select an available date and time slot from the calendar. *Tip: New booking slots typically release daily at 3:30 PM to 4:00 PM NST.*

### Step 3: Enter Personal Data & Verified NID
Input your verified 10-digit NID Number. The portal automatically syncs and populates your legal name, date of birth, and parents' details from civil registration records. Verify all spellings against your Nepali Citizenship Certificate.

### Step 4: Upload Clear Scanned Documents
Upload clear scanned copies (under 300 KB each) of:
- Original Nepali Citizenship Certificate (Front and Back).
- Old Passport bio-page (for renewals).
- Marriage certificate (if surname changed post-marriage).

### Step 5: Download and Print the Barcode Application Slip
Upon submission, download and print the generated PDF application receipt containing your unique **Application Number and QR/Barcode**. You must present this physical slip during your biometric verification appointment.

## What to Expect on Appointment Day (Biometric Station)

On your scheduled appointment date:
1. **Arrive 30 minutes early** at your chosen DAO or DoP Tripureshwor.
2. Present your printed barcode application slip, original citizenship certificate, and payment voucher.
3. Complete live biometric capture:
   - High-definition digital facial photo (wear dark clothes; avoid white shirts as the background is white).
   - Digital fingerprint scanning.
   - Electronic signature pad signing.
4. The verification officer reviews your physical documents and issues an acknowledgment token.

## How to Track Your E-Passport Delivery Status

You can monitor production progress online:
1. Visit [nepalpassport.gov.np](https://nepalpassport.gov.np) and click **Track Application**.
2. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth.
3. Status messages include:
   - *Under Verification*: Documents under review.
   - *Sent for Personalization*: Chip printing in progress.
   - *Dispatched*: In transit to your local district office.
   - *Ready for Collection*: Available for pickup. Bring your token and original citizenship to collect your e-Passport.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I apply for an urgent e-Passport directly in Kathmandu if my citizenship is from another district?
Yes. Any citizen can apply for an urgent e-Passport (NPR 12,000) at the central Department of Passports in Tripureshwor, regardless of home district, provided their National ID (NID) is verified.

### Can minors (under 16) get an e-Passport without a National ID?
Yes. Children under 16 do not require an NID. Instead, they require an official **Minor Identity Card (Nabalak Parichayapatra)** issued by their DAO, an official birth certificate, and copies of both parents' citizenship certificates.
