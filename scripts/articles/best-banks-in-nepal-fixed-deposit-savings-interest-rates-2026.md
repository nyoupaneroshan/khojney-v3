## Key Factors When Choosing a Bank in Nepal

### 1. Base Rate and Loan Spreads
If you plan to take a Home Loan (Ghar Karja) or Auto Loan, a lower **Base Rate** is critical. Loan interest rates in Nepal are priced as:
The formula is: \`Interest Rate = Bank Base Rate + Premium Spread (e.g., 1.5% to 3.0%)\`.
Banks like Rastriya Banijya Bank (RBB) and Standard Chartered traditionally maintain lower base rates, resulting in cheaper long-term borrowing costs.

### 2. Digital Banking & Mobile App UX
Mobile banking in Nepal powers daily utility bills, Fonepay QR merchant transactions, and ConnectIPS transfers. Banks with modern, reliable apps (e.g., Nabil SmartBank, NIC Asia MoBank, Global Smart Plus) minimize the need for physical branch visits.

### 3. ATM and Branch Accessibility
Ensure the bank maintains functional ATMs and branches near your residence or workplace to avoid inter-bank ATM fees.

## How to Open an Online Bank Account in Nepal

Most commercial banks allow paperless online account opening:
1. Visit the bank's official portal and select **Open Account Online**.
2. Complete the online Know Your Customer (KYC) form with your citizenship details, permanent address, and biometric photos.
3. Select your desired account scheme (e.g., Remittance Savings, Student Savings, Senior Citizen Account).
4. Verify your identity via Video KYC (V-KYC) or visit the nearest branch for physical signature verification.
5. Receive your account number and activate mobile banking instantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the maximum insurance coverage on bank deposits in Nepal?
Under Nepal's Deposit and Credit Guarantee Fund (DCGF), individual retail savings and fixed deposits are insured up to **NPR 5,00,000 per depositor** per licensed financial institution.

### What documents are required to open a bank account in Nepal?
- Original and photocopy of your Nepali Citizenship Certificate (or Passport).
- Two recent passport-size photographs.
- Utility bill (electricity, water, or internet) for address verification.
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) card (mandatory for large transaction volumes).
