# Complete Guide to Scholarships in Nepal 2026: Government, University & Foreign Funded

Higher education costs in Nepal have risen across professional fields like Medicine, Engineering, Information Technology, and Business Management. Fortunately, thousands of fully funded **scholarships in Nepal**, government quotas, and merit tuition waivers are awarded annually by the **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST)**, national universities, and bilateral international partner foundations.

This guide provides a roadmap to securing scholarships in Nepal: how to qualify for government quota seats, crack competitive entrance waivers, and access international opportunities.

---

## Major Categories of Scholarships Available to Nepali Students

Scholarship opportunities for Nepali students are structured into four main categories:

1. **Government Quota Scholarships (MoEST)**: Mandated by federal legislation, all private and public colleges in Nepal must allocate 10% to 20% of their total seats as subsidized or free scholarship quotas for eligible candidates.
2. **University Competitive Merit Scholarships**: Awarded directly by Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu University, and Pokhara University based on rank in standardized entrance exams.
3. **Inclusivity & Reservation Quotas**: Dedicated scholarship allocations for women, marginalized communities (Dalit, Janajati, Madhesi, Tharu, Muslim), differently-abled students, and remote districts (Karnali and Far-West).
4. **Bilateral & International Grants**: Full funding offered by foreign governments (India, China, Japan, USA, UK, Australia) to Nepali scholars.

## Top Institutional Scholarships in Nepal

### 1. Medical Education Commission (MEC / CEE) MBBS Free Seats
Under Nepal's Medical Education Act, public medical colleges (IOM Maharajgunj, BPKIHS Dharan, PAHS Lalitpur, Karnali Academy) provide free tuition for top rankers in the Common Entrance Examination (CEE). Private medical colleges are also legally required to reserve **10% of their MBBS seats for free scholarship candidates**.
- **Award Value**: Up to NPR 45 Lakhs to 55 Lakhs in tuition fees.
- **Selection**: Purely determined by merit rank in the [MBBS CEE Entrance Exam](/exams/mbbs-cee).

### 2. IOE Pulchowk Engineering Regular Seats
The Institute of Engineering (IOE) at Tribhuvan University awards heavily subsidized "Regular" seats to top scorers in the IOE Entrance Examination across its constituent campuses (Pulchowk, Thapathali, Paschimanchal, Purwanchal, Chitwan).
- **Tuition Cost**: Less than NPR 35,000 for the entire 4-year Bachelor of Engineering degree.
- **Preparation**: Practice with our free [IOE Entrance Mock Tests](/exams/ioe-entrance).

### 3. Kathmandu University (KU) Need-Based & Merit Scholarships
Kathmandu University offers the **KU-Financial Aid** scheme, covering 50% to 100% of tuition fees for deserving students in Engineering, Science, and Management who demonstrate financial need.

## Checklist to Win a Higher Education Scholarship

- [ ] **Maintain Strong Academic Transcripts**: Consistently high GPAs in SEE and +2 examinations form the baseline for shortlisting.
- [ ] **Prepare for Competitive Entrance Exams Early**: High ranks in exams like CEE, IOE, and CMAT are direct tickets to tuition waivers.
- [ ] **Gather Inclusivity & Financial Verification Papers**: Secure certified recommendation letters from your local Ward Office confirming economic hardship or community status.
- [ ] **Write a Compelling Statement of Purpose (SOP)**: Clearly articulate your career trajectory and how your education will contribute to Nepal's socio-economic development.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the government scholarship bond in Nepal?
Students who receive 100% free government scholarship seats in professional programs like MBBS or Engineering are typically required to sign a service bond committing to work in designated government district hospitals or public development projects for **1 to 2 years** post-graduation.
