# NEPSE Share Market Guide 2026: How to Open Demat, Apply IPO & Trade in Nepal

The **Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE)** is the sole regulated stock exchange in Nepal, facilitating capital mobilization and secondary market trading for commercial banks, hydropower companies, microfinance institutions, mutual funds, and manufacturing corporations. With over 6.2 million Demat accounts and rapid digitization through **MeroShare** and online **Trade Management Systems (TMS)**, investing in the **NEPSE share market in Nepal** has become one of the most accessible and rewarding avenues for long-term wealth creation.

Whether you are a beginner looking to submit your very first 10-kitta Initial Public Offering (IPO) application or an aspiring trader looking to understand stock broker accounts, this comprehensive guide covers the end-to-end investment process in Nepal.

---

## What is NEPSE and How Does the Nepal Stock Market Work?

The **Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE)** operates under the direct regulatory supervision of the **Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON)**. Listed companies on NEPSE are categorized into primary sectors including Commercial Banks, Development Banks, Finance, Hydropower, Life Insurance, Non-Life Insurance, Microfinance, Hotels & Tourism, Manufacturing & Processing, and Investment.

Trading occurs electronically through licensed brokerage firms via the automated NEPSE Trading System (NOTS / TMS). Trading hours run from **Sunday to Thursday between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM (NST)**, with pre-open sessions from 10:30 AM to 10:45 AM.

## Primary Market (IPO) vs Secondary Market in Nepal

Understanding the distinction between primary and secondary markets is fundamental before deploying capital:

| Parameter | Primary Market (IPO / FPO) | Secondary Market (TMS Trading) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Company issues fresh shares directly to the public | Investors buy and sell existing shares amongst themselves |
| **Price** | Fixed face value (typically NPR 100 per unit in Nepal) | Fluctuates dynamically based on supply and demand |
| **Allotment** | Lottery / Prorated by CDSC (10-kitta minimum rule) | Guaranteed execution if buy/sell prices match |
| **Risk Level** | Low to Moderate | Moderate to High (Market volatility) |
| **Platform** | MeroShare Portal / Mobile App | Broker TMS (Trade Management System) |

## 4 Essential Prerequisites to Start Investing in Nepal

To buy shares in Nepal, you need four key accounts:

### 1. Bank Account with C-ASBA Facility
You must hold a savings account in any "A", "B", or "C" class commercial bank or financial institution licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank. Ensure your bank supports **C-ASBA (Centralized Application Supported by Blocked Amount)**. Compare high-yield savings accounts using our [Nepal Banks Guide](/banks).

### 2. Demat Account (Beneficiary Owner ID - BOID)
A Demat (Dematerialized) account holds your shares electronically, eliminating physical paper certificates. It consists of a 16-digit BOID number. Demat accounts can be opened at any commercial bank, merchant banker (Capital), or licensed stock broker.

### 3. MeroShare Account
Managed by **CDS and Clearing Limited (CDSC)**, MeroShare is the centralized online portal ([meroshare.cdsc.com.np](https://meroshare.cdsc.com.np)) used to apply for IPOs, FPOs, right shares, and transfer shares via Electronic Delivery Instruction Slip (EDIS).

### 4. CRN Number (C-ASBA Registration Number)
Your bank issues a unique CRN number upon verifying your Demat account with your bank details. This code authorizes MeroShare to temporarily block funds in your bank account when applying for an IPO.

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply for an IPO in Nepal via MeroShare

Applying for an IPO takes less than two minutes once your accounts are active:

1. **Log in to MeroShare**: Navigate to [meroshare.cdsc.com.np](https://meroshare.cdsc.com.np) or open the MeroShare mobile app. Select your depository participant (DP), enter your username, and password.
2. **Go to 'My ASBA'**: In the left sidebar navigation, click on **My ASBA** and navigate to the **Apply for Issue** tab.
3. **Select Active IPO**: You will see all open public issues. Click the **Apply** button next to the desired company.
4. **Fill Application Details**:
   - Choose your Bank Name and Account Number.
   - Enter your applied unit count (The standard minimum lot in Nepal is **10 shares**, costing NPR 1,000 at par value).
   - Enter your bank-provided **CRN Number**.
5. **Accept Declaration & Enter Pin**: Read the disclosure terms, enter your 4-digit transaction PIN, and click **Submit**.
6. **Verify Application Status**: Under **Application Report**, verify that your status shows *Unverified* initially, followed by *Verified* once your bank blocks the required amount.

## How to Trade on the Secondary Market (Opening a Broker TMS Account)

If you wish to buy and sell already-listed shares, you must register with a registered stock broker:

### Steps to Open an Online TMS Account:
1. Visit the online TMS portal of your chosen broker (for example, Broker No. 58 or Broker No. 45).
2. Click **New Registration / Client Registration**.
3. Submit personal identification (Citizenship Certificate, recent passport photo, PAN card, Demat confirmation, and handwritten signature).
4. Upon broker verification (usually 24–48 hours), you will receive an activation email with your TMS Client Code and temporary password.
5. Log in, deposit trading collateral via ConnectIPS or digital wallets, and place your Buy or Sell orders between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

## Taxes and Transaction Fees in NEPSE

When trading on the secondary market in Nepal, keep these statutory charges in mind:

| Cost Component | Charge Rate | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEBON Regulatory Fee** | 0.015% | Applied on transaction volume |
| **Broker Commission** | 0.27% - 0.40% | Tiered based on transaction amount |
| **DP Fee** | NPR 5 per transaction | Applied when transferring shares |
| **Capital Gains Tax (Individual)** | 5.0% (Holding > 365 days) / 7.5% (Holding < 365 days) | Levied strictly on net profit |
| **Capital Gains Tax (Corporate)** | 10.0% | Levied on institutional profits |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the minimum capital required to start investing in Nepal?
You can start with as little as **NPR 1,000** by applying for 10 units of an ordinary share IPO at the face value of NPR 100 per share.

### What is the 10-kitta allotment rule in Nepal?
Under SEBON guidelines, every valid applicant is allocated a minimum of 10 shares through a randomized lottery system until all available shares are distributed.

### How long does it take for IPO allotment results to appear on MeroShare?
Allotments are typically declared within 3 to 7 working days following issue closure. Results can be verified on MeroShare under *Application Report* or via [iporesult.cdsc.com.np](https://iporesult.cdsc.com.np).

### Can Nepali citizens living abroad invest in NEPSE?
Yes. Non-Resident Nepalis (NRNs) and foreign migrant workers can open Remittance Demat and C-ASBA accounts to participate in the reserved 10% foreign employment quota for all public IPOs.
